## Key Learning Points

### Network Topologies

I learned about several common network topologies and their advantages and disadvantages:

- **Star Topology** - Devices connect to a central switch or hub. This is the most common topology used today due to its scalability and reliability.
- **Bus Topology** - Devices share a single backbone cable, making it cost-effective but introducing a single point of failure.
- **Ring Topology** - Devices communicate through a circular path, providing structured communication between connected systems.

### Subnetting

The room introduced the concept of subnetting and how large networks can be divided into smaller, more manageable segments.

Key benefits include:

- Improved network performance
- Better organisation of IP address ranges
- Reduced broadcast traffic
- Increased security and control

### Address Resolution Protocol (ARP)

I explored how ARP is used to map IP addresses to physical MAC addresses so that devices can communicate on a local network.
