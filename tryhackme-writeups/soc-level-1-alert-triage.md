# TryHackMe - SOC Level 1 - Alert Triage

## Overview

This room focused on the process of investigating and triaging security alerts within a Security Operations Centre (SOC) environment. The exercises demonstrated how analysts assess alerts, gather evidence, determine severity, and decide whether escalation or further investigation is required.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Alert triage
- Security event investigation
- Log analysis
- Incident categorisation
- Severity assessment
- False positive identification
- Evidence gathering
- Escalation decision making

## Concepts Covered

### Alert Validation

Validated security alerts to determine whether activity was legitimate, suspicious, or malicious.

### Prioritisation

Assessed alerts based on potential business impact, likelihood of compromise, and severity.

### Investigation

Reviewed available evidence to understand the nature and scope of the activity.

### Escalation

Determined when incidents required escalation to senior analysts or incident responders.

## Tools and Technologies

- SIEM concepts
- Log analysis
- Event investigation workflows

## Key Learning Outcomes

After completing this room I can:

- Follow a structured alert triage process
- Distinguish false positives from genuine security events
- Assess alert severity and impact
- Gather and document investigative evidence
- Communicate findings in a clear and concise manner

## Relevance to a SOC Analyst Role

Alert triage is a core responsibility of Tier 1 SOC Analysts. This room provided practical experience in analysing alerts, investigating suspicious activity, and supporting incident response processes.

## Reflection

This room reinforced the importance of following a repeatable investigation methodology. I learned that effective alert triage is not simply reviewing alerts but involves validating evidence, understanding context, prioritising risk, and making appropriate escalation decisions.
