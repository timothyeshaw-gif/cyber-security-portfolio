I completed the TryHackMe Introductory DFIR (Digital Forensics and Incident Response) module to develop my understanding of how organisations investigate and respond to cyber security incidents.

The room introduced the core concepts of Digital Forensics and Incident Response, including evidence preservation, incident investigation, containment, recovery, root cause analysis, and lessons learned.

## Key Learning Points

### Digital Forensics

Digital forensics focuses on collecting, preserving and analysing evidence following a cyber security incident. The integrity of evidence must be maintained to support investigations and potential legal proceedings.

### Incident Response

Incident response provides a structured approach to managing security incidents. Typical stages include:

1. Preparation
2. Detection and Analysis
3. Containment
4. Eradication
5. Recovery
6. Lessons Learned

### Root Cause Analysis

Understanding why an incident occurred is as important as resolving it. Techniques such as the 5 Whys can help investigators identify underlying weaknesses in systems, processes and controls.

### Post-Incident Reviews

Post-mortem reviews help organisations learn from incidents by:
- Identifying root causes
- Assessing the effectiveness of the response
- Capturing lessons learned
- Recommending improvements
- Reducing the likelihood of recurrence

## Outcome

This room strengthened my understanding of how incident response and digital forensics work together to manage and learn from security incidents. It also reinforced the importance of evidence gathering, documentation, timelines and continuous improvement following an incident.
