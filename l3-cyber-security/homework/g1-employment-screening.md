# 📝 G1 Homework — Employment Screening Checks

This homework covers the core checks, tools, red flags, and discrepancy-handling steps used during employee screening in cyber‑related or sensitive roles.

---

## 1. Types of Checks to Conduct

- **Identity verification**  
  Confirms legal name, address, and identity documents.

- **Right to work check**  
  Ensures the candidate is legally allowed to work in the UK.

- **Criminal record check (DBS)**  
  Used only when the role is eligible under safeguarding or security law.  
  Levels include Basic, Standard, and Enhanced.

- **Employment history verification**  
  Confirms previous roles, dates, responsibilities, and reasons for leaving.

- **Education and qualification checks**  
  Verifies degrees, certificates, and professional qualifications.

- **Credit or financial checks**  
  Used for roles involving money handling, financial responsibility, or sensitive data.

- **Security clearance (BPSS, SC, DV)**  
  Required for government, defence, or national‑security‑related positions.

---

## 2. Verification Tools or Services

- **DBS Online** — criminal record checks  
- **Home Office Right to Work Portal** — RTW verification  
- **Experian / Equifax** — credit and financial checks  
- **Gov.uk Verify / Yoti** — identity validation  
- **Direct contact with universities or employers** — reference checks  
- **BPSS / UKSV** — government security clearance verification

---

## 3. Red Flags to Watch For

- Inconsistent employment dates or unexplained gaps  
- Refusal to provide documentation or references  
- Vague job descriptions or unverifiable qualifications  
- Evidence of falsified or altered documents  
- Aggressive or defensive behaviour when asked for proof

---

## 4. Handling Discrepancies

- Pause the recruitment process and request clarification or evidence  
- Document all findings, communications, and concerns  
- Escalate serious issues to HR or compliance  
- Withdraw the offer and report appropriately if fraud is confirmed  
- Review internal procedures to prevent similar issues in future

---
